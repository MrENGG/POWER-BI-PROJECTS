Power BI Projects:
A collection of end-to-end data analytics and business intelligence dashboards built using Microsoft Power BI, showcasing expertise in data modeling, multi-page report design, geospatial mapping, sports analytics, retail intelligence, and telecommunications metrics.

### Dashboard Overview:

**1. MotoGP Riders Performance Dashboard**
* **Domain:** Sports Analytics (Motorsport)[cite: 1]
* **Description:** An exploratory analysis of rider performance, constructor dynamics, and historical trends across MotoGP racing classes[cite: 1].
* **Key Features:** Rider count and points breakdown by racing class (MotoGP, Moto2, Moto3, etc.), geographical distribution analyzing points by home country, multi-class motorcycle correlations, and a seasonal timeline tracking total points evolution[cite: 1].

**2. Formula 1 Circuit Analysis**
* **Domain:** Sports Analytics & Geospatial Mapping[cite: 2]
* **Description:** A spatial and structural breakdown mapping out the world's 78 Formula 1 circuits across 35 countries[cite: 2].
* **Key Features:** High-level KPI cards tracking total circuits (78), country counts (35), location counts (75), and cumulative altitude; altitude profiling identifying highest-elevation circuits; country-wise hosting distribution[cite: 2].

**3. Retail Sales Dashboard**
* **Domain:** Enterprise Business Intelligence & Commerce[cite: 3]
* **Description:** A robust commercial analytics dashboard analyzing $8.25M in total sales, tracking order fulfillment, regional performance, and customer behavior[cite: 3].
* **Key Features:** Order status pipelines (Completed, Shipped, Returned, Cancelled) and multi-year sales trends (2022–2025); regional and city-level sales distribution; hierarchical treemaps for product categories/subcategories and customer segment donut charts[cite: 3].

**4. Samsung Performance & 5G Analytics Suite**
* **Domain:** Telecommunications & Enterprise Tech Intelligence[cite: 4, 5, 6]
* **Description:** A comprehensive, multi-page business intelligence report analyzing Samsung’s global market footprint, revenue drivers, and next-generation 5G connectivity[cite: 4, 5, 6].
* **Key Features:** Executive Overview ($30.2B in revenue, 33M units sold)[cite: 4]; Product & Regional Analysis (model-specific revenues for Galaxy S and A series)[cite: 5]; 5G & Advanced Analysis (network speeds, regional 5G coverage percentages, and user preferences)[cite: 6].
